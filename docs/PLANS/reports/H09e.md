## Deviations from the plan

1. **`EditorLink` struct not created in `ui/editor_views/`.** The plan's alternative is used
   instead: the free fn `cached_editor(cached, &base, &path)` (the plan explicitly sanctions it:
   "or calls a free fn `cached_editor(cached, &base, &path)`") plus `call_cached_editor`. Two
   concrete blockers for `get(&mut self, base)`:
   - `editor()`/`call_editor()` are called from `&self` helpers in three of the four classes
     (`MindMapView::{tool,set_tool,tile_at,unproject}`, `MindMapObjectivesCanvas::{objectives,
     valid_place,node_at,node_x,node_y}`, `MindWaveGraph::wave_graph_data`); an `&mut self` `get`
     would force ~10 receiver changes up those chains.
   - `call_editor(&self)` holds the class's shared bind guard across `editor.call(method, …)`
     (a re-entrant Godot call). Turning it into `&mut self` would make that a `bind_mut` guard
     across the re-entry, which is the "already bound" panic shape RULES §4 forbids. Keeping the
     `&self` shape is the safe reading.
   The cache is also read-only (no write-back of a re-resolved node), matching the old
   `resolve_editor` exactly.

2. **`with_world`'s closure takes three arguments, not two.** The plan's
   `f: impl FnOnce(&mut WorldEditorGrid, &ContentRegistry)` cannot be used at any of the 14 sites:
   every one of them also calls `self.editor.<method>(…)` *and* several call `self.mark_tiles_changed()`
   right after the world is dropped. `&mut self` + a two-argument closure would forbid both. The
   implemented signature hands the destructured `&mut MapEditor` in as the first closure argument,
   which still collapses each 9-line `WorldEditorGrid::new(…)+call` block into one call; adoption
   count is the plan's 14. Ordering: `WorldEditorGrid::new` is a pure view constructor (only stores
   the five references, `mind-core/src/editor/grid.rs:38-54`), so in `dev_draw_rect`/`dev_draw_circle`
   the brush-size save/restore now happens inside the closure after the view is built; the
   observable mutation order (brush set → draws → `flush_op` → brush restored) is unchanged, and the
   content guard still returns `false` before any mutation.

3. **`with_connector` adopted at 5 of the 15 `let Some(connector) = self.connector.as_mut() else`
   sites.** The other ten bodies also use another field (`self.session`, `self.sender`,
   `self.runtime`, `self.sim_host`) or another `&mut self` method, which the helper's `&mut self`
   borrow cannot express (a closure passed to it may not touch `self`):
   `net/actions.rs:56` (warn + `self.publish_call_packet`), `net/drain.rs:89,111`,
   `net/mod.rs:578,649,806,843,868,877,1317` (host/join/leave/start/ready/request_snapshot).
   They keep the local `let … else` shape. A `with_session`-shaped helper could take six of them,
   but that is a new API the package does not list — left for C01 if wanted.

4. **`value_gstring`: 12 sites, not 50.** The package's "50" is the total `GString::from` count in
   `net/` at `291e30a8`; `value_gstring` takes `&serde_json::Value`, so only the 12 conversions from
   a `Value` match its body. The other 38 are string literals, `String`/`&str`/`format!` output,
   dictionary keys or node names, which are not `Value` serializations.

5. **`ui/root/`: 12 readers, not 8+8.** The 8 `try_to::<GString>().ok()` chains: 7 are adopted; the
   eighth (`messages.rs` icon) falls back to `GString::from("ok")`, which `dict_text_try_or`'s `""`
   would change (RULES §4: differing fallback keeps its local copy). The `try_to::<bool>()` sites
   split into 3 dictionary reads (adopted via `dict_bool_try_or`) and 5 `Object::call(…)` results
   (`escape.rs`, `mod.rs:434`, `ui_scale.rs:70,154`) which are not dictionaries; `gdutil`'s
   `to_bool_*` helpers have different fallbacks, so those stay. Two neighbouring relaxed reads
   (`accessors.rs` sector i64, `ui_scale.rs` value f64) had the exact `dict_*_relaxed_or` bodies and
   were adopted too.

6. **`set_load_error` covers `@editor.errorload` only.** 12 pairs adopted. The two
   `@editor.errorimage` pairs (`api_lifecycle.rs`) and two `@editor.sectorgenerate` pairs
   (`api_filters.rs`) use a different key; a keyed helper would be a new API, so they keep the
   literal pair. `set_map_error` (MapError) is untouched.

7. **`content()` at 25 sites; 7 `self.content.as_ref()` uses stay** (plus the 2 helper bodies):
   `api_filters.rs:22` and `mod.rs:378` are `match` shapes, `api_objectives.rs:59` is an inline
   argument, `mod.rs:223` (`bootstrap`) inserts the block table into `self.ecs` while the registry is
   borrowed, and `api_filters.rs:50` / `api_lifecycle.rs:99` pass `content` together with
   `&mut self.grid`/`&mut self.editor` — a `&self` accessor borrows all of `self`, so those two need
   the field-level borrow. `mod.rs:255/293` are the new helper bodies and `mod.rs:318` is
   `content_ref`'s body (already a same-shaped `pub(crate)` seam; left verbatim).
