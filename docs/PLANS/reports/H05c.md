## Deviations from the plan

1. **The three common helpers are re-exported from `util.rs` instead of imported at each
   call site.** `rich`, `ui_call` and `defer_action` have no local body any more; the
   `pub(in crate::ui::dialogs) use` lines keep the group's `use util::*` globs resolving.
   C01 deletes this leaf and repoints those imports at `dialogs::common`.
2. **`flat_box` and `notify_key` needed a name each** (see above): the merge puts two
   differing bodies in one module, so a second name was unavoidable. No body changed.
3. **`DeferredAction` (util_f) and util_e's `REQUEST_META` were deleted** together with the
   copies that used them; grep shows no other user in the crate.
4. **The refresh meta key changed** from `mind_batch_e_request` to the shared
   `mind_dialog_request`, as package step 6 requires. Only the old `editor/util_e.rs` named
   the old key, and every writer/reader moved to `MindDialog`'s trio in the same commit.
5. **util_d's dead `parse_json_object` was deleted** (its body is identical to
   `gdutil::variant::parse_object_trimmed`, which remains). Nothing else dead was removed.
6. **Base is `4e33a65a`, not the plan's `291e30a8`** (W1 merged). The helper leaves already
   existed with the contents the plan describes; no plan item was missing or extra.
7. **`editor/mod.rs` was edited for its `pub mod` lines only** — allowed by package step 4
   ("add and remove files and `pub mod` lines inside the group you own"); its dispatch chain
   is untouched.
