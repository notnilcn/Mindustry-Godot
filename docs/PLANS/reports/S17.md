## Deviations from the plan

- **Part A `spec.rs` is two files.** The plan asks for `spec.rs` 681–2,169 and says "if it is over
  1500, put 681–1222 into `types.rs`". The source slice is 1,488 lines; with its new header/imports
  the file would have been 1,496 lines — under, but directly at, the RULES §3 1,500-line ceiling,
  and both the package's *Owns* list and its `move_check --new` list name `types.rs`. I therefore
  applied the plan's own fallback at the line it names (after `BarSpec`, line 1,216): `types.rs`
  679–1,216 (547 lines) and `spec.rs` 1,218–2,167 (963 lines). Same items, same verbatim text.
- **Part A range edges move to the item prefix.** `kind.rs` starts at 62 (not 67) to keep
  `BlockKind`'s doc comment; `def.rs` starts at 2,385 (`BlockDef` doc + derive); `loader.rs` starts
  at 3,841 (`BlockSink` doc). Range ends were trimmed of the blank line that separates items.
- **Part A `consume.rs` contains `block_under_bullets`, `spec()` and `rgba_hex`** because the plan's
  literal 2,170–2,386 range covers them (they are not `consume_*` builders). They are builders/colour
  helpers for generated specs, so leaving them there matches the range rather than the prose.
  `ui_round_amount`/`round_to` likewise land in `traits.rs` by the plan's 3,526–3,841 range.
- **Part B range edges.** `types.rs` starts at 57 (the `ItemAmmo` derive), `config.rs` at 566 (the
  `// ---- content registration` section comment), `update.rs` ends at 2,270 and
  `build_turret_efficiency`'s doc (2,271–2,273) moves with it into `build_turret.rs` (the plan cut at
  2,273, mid-doc), `targeting.rs` starts at 2,804 (`TurretBuild.shoot` doc), `tests.rs` ends at 3,951.
- **Part C range edges.** `mod.rs` is 1–172 (the decoded records end at 172; 173 is the
  `LiveEntitySink` doc). `decode.rs` ends at 904 (`decode_bytes_inner`); `meta_tick` and
  `SectorResumeMeta` (906–931) move to `handler.rs`, which is their topic per the plan's Contents
  column ("meta tags, SimMapSource, SimIoHandler") even though its line range starts at 1,244.
  `handler.rs` therefore concatenates two source slices, in source order.
- **Visibility.** All formerly private top-level items of the three files became `pub(super)`
  (RULES §3.2): `COLOR_*`/`PAL_ACCENT`/`TEAM_CRUX`, `resolve_item`/`resolve_consume`,
  `NamesSink`/`RegistrySink`/`load_names`; 57 turret helpers/consts; `LiveEntitySink`,
  `LiveEntitySource`, `ai_kind_*`, `unit_plan_*`, `live_unit_rows`, `decode_bytes_inner`,
  `live_meta_tags`, `SimMapSource`, `boot_registry`. Fields of moved structs that moved code reads
  from a sibling got the same treatment: `DecodedSave::entities`, `LiveEntitySink::{units,fires,
  puddles}` and `LiveEntitySource::sim`. Items already `pub`/`pub(crate)` kept their visibility
  exactly; `set_unit_elevation` was already `pub(super)`.
- **Sibling imports.** Each new file has `use super::*;` (blocks) or explicit `super::…` imports and
  absolute `crate::…` paths (RULES §3.3); cross-file private helpers are imported explicitly
  (`use super::math::{…}`, `use super::ammo::{…}`, `use super::entities::LiveEntitySink`, …). Two
  relative references were kept resolvable by importing the module rather than rewriting the call
  site: `advanced::config_for`/`advanced::register_bullets` (config.rs), `advanced::update_*`
  (update.rs) now come from `use crate::world::blocks::defense::turrets::advanced;`.
- **No behaviour edit.** No ECS iteration order, sort key, float expression shape, RNG call order,
  content id or `add_systems` order changed; all goldens/checksums are identical.
