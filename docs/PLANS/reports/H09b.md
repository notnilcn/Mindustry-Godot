## Deviations from the plan

1. **`push_reset` takes `FnOnce`, not `Fn`.** The plan's item 4 shows
   `impl Fn(&mut ContentRegistry) + 'static`, but `ResetAction` is
   `Box<dyn FnOnce(&mut ContentRegistry) + 'static>` and 12 of the 17 closures move a non-`Copy`
   original into the target (`unit.weapons = original`, `block.requirements = original`, …), so
   they only implement `FnOnce`. `FnOnce` is the strictly more general bound and keeps every call
   site identical.
2. **Two extra optional helpers.** The plan names `set_opt_f32s` and annotates the
   `*target = Some(value)` shape as 23×. That 23 is the total of all optional-typed loops at this
   base: 10 `Option<f32>`, 5 `Option<i32>`, 8 `Option<bool>`. I added `set_opt_i32s`/
   `set_opt_bools` (differently named, as RULES §4 allows for a differing type) and adopted all 23
   rather than leaving 13 local copies.
3. **Loop count.** The plan says 71 loops; there are 72 `for (field, target)` loops at this base
   and 55 have a typed-field-reader body (16+5+11 plain, 10+5+8 optional). The 17 others are the
   transform loops listed above. Counts drifted with later J02/L06 field additions; the plan's
   per-helper counts are otherwise consistent (19 `parse_err` sites confirmed).
4. **`set_scalar` is private**, as the plan says ("generic fn"), while the three wrappers stay
   `pub(super)` with unchanged signatures. No external path changed.
5. `macro_rules! parse_err` lives in `json/mod.rs` rather than `fields.rs`: placing it above the
   child `mod` declarations gives every child textual scope without 10 extra `use` lines.
